Terms of Use

Last updated: October 2026

Purpose of this service

MediShare is a community accessibility and review platform. It is not a pharmacy, healthcare provider, prescriber, drug distributor or emergency service.

No medical advice or safety guarantee

Information, images, review statuses and messages are not medical advice and do not establish that a medicine is genuine, safe, suitable for any person, or lawful to transfer. Do not start, stop, share, or substitute medicines based on this platform. Consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Responsible participation

Provide accurate information and only upload images and details you are authorized to share. Do not list, request, offer, or arrange the transfer of prescription-only, controlled, expired, recalled, compromised, improperly stored or otherwise restricted medicines. Do not use messages or off-platform contact to evade platform safeguards.

Review is not transfer authorization

A platform reviewer’s status is an administrative screening outcome, not professional treatment advice or legal approval. Any medicine transfer must follow applicable laws and be conducted only through an authorized pathway. Request workflows are disabled by default outside local development and must not be enabled without legal and qualified pharmacy-professional approval.

Account and content

Keep your account credentials secure. You remain responsible for the accuracy and lawful sharing of information you submit. We may restrict submissions or accounts when necessary to protect people or respond to a safety concern.

Prototype status

This software is an early-stage MVP. Some integrations, including mobile OTP, real-time messaging, identity checks and automated recall data, are not available. Do not rely on this prototype for healthcare or medicine distribution decisions.
