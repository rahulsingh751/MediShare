A few answers

Questions, answered.

Clear expectations help build a safer community.

Can I both submit medicines and search for them?

Yes. Every verified member can use both workflows. There are no separate permanent donor or receiver account types.

Does approval mean a medicine is safe to use?

No. Platform review is not a medical evaluation, and a photograph cannot establish safety. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about medicines.

Can I request prescription or controlled medicines?

No. These are restricted from discovery and request workflows in this MVP. Do not use the platform to arrange a transfer outside authorized channels.

Is email or mobile verification fully configured?

Email verification works with Django's configured email backend. The default local backend prints the link in the terminal. SMS OTP needs a vetted provider and credentials before it can be enabled.
